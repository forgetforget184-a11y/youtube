# Crayon Capital — Reference Breakdown (from watching the 3 reference videos)

> User overrides (win over this breakdown): clean lines with no wiggle, characters must act out every clause, ~15-minute scripts, music bed at 10–20 % of the voice.

Sources: eS1zZKHFzwE (Nvidia/Jensen), tsZIyy18qIo (J.P. Morgan), rkJ9v-GJzLw (Steve Jobs). Analysed with video analysis. Where the analyses disagreed, the more specific frame-level answer (J.P. Morgan follow-up) wins.

## Characters
- Stick-figure hybrids with BIG circular heads (head roughly as tall as the body). No neck: head sits right on the shoulders.
- Torso = simple rounded shape (filled clothing color, e.g. navy suit, black turtleneck, light-blue shirt). Arms = single thick lines, NO hands (line just ends). Legs = two thin stick lines ending in small rounded shoe blobs.
- Uniform medium black/dark-grey outline, rounded caps. The originals have a slight line boil, BUT the user wants CLEAN, stable lines — do NOT reproduce the boil (engine default CC.BOIL=false).
- Face: two black dot eyes, single-stroke arc mouth (smile/frown/flat/small "O" for shock), single short floating eyebrow lines that tilt for emotion. Eyes widen for shock.
- Hair = solid filled cap shape (black/white/brown) on top of the head. Identity comes from hair, hats (top hat), mustache, glasses, clothing color. ~10–15 reusable base rigs.
- Emotion symbols: sweat drops, blue tear streaks, red anger vein marks / face flush, shock lines, lightbulb pop over head, thought bubbles, speech bubbles.

## Character animation
- Puppet/cut-out: limbs rotate on pivots; poses SNAP (little tweening). Animated "on 2s/3s" (stepped ~8–12 fps feel inside a 24/30 fps video).
- Characters mostly SLIDE across the ground (no real walk cycle); a small bob while sliding is fine. Scale down to suggest walking into distance.
- Mouth flaps are minimal or absent in some videos; speech bubbles are used for dialogue. Blinks rare.
- Interactions: carrying objects (prop parented to arm), handing over documents, pointing (torso rotates slightly), kneeling/collapsing, sitting (body drops onto chair), handshakes (slide together), writing with quill (back-and-forth stroke).
- Squash & stretch on jumps/falls/impacts.

## Objects / props
- Enter with POP (scale 0→1 with slight overshoot) or SLIDE in from off-screen; text/lines/charts DRAW ON (stroke reveal).
- Exit by pop-out, slide-off, or hard cut. Transform by sprite swap (rifle → broken rifle, cash pile → graph), cracks forming, stamps ("HIRED") slamming onto paper, coins dropping into a jar and jiggling, newspapers flapping, documents shaking when held.
- Typical props: skyscraper/bank building, globe with building on top, mountain with cracks, money bags, coin stacks, jars, checks, newspapers, resumes, stamps, quill pens, chips/computers, phones, charts, thought-bubble contents.

## Backgrounds & palette
- Muted, desaturated flat colors (light blue sky, office tan, dark grey, beige), subtle grain/crayon texture; sometimes warm paper off-white; dramatic beats use black background + spotlight.
- Very flat perspective; buildings = rectangles with window grids.

## Camera & transitions
- Camera mostly STATIC. Emphasis via smooth push-in on a face/prop for punchlines; occasional slight zoom on establishing shots.
- Transitions: almost all HARD CUTS; occasional whip pan between locations; fade to black for chapter cards.
- Chapter title cards: white text on black (e.g. "The Making of a Weapon").

## Text
- Handwritten font, short labels/callouts, sometimes in rectangular callout boxes; can typewriter-in; keep text stable (no boil).

## Sound
- Pop on every object entrance, whoosh on slides/whips, scribble on draw-ons/writing, coin clink/cash, stamp thud, ding for success, paper rustle. Music: upbeat quirky plucky/pizzicato/tuba-percussion bed, low under VO; mystery tone for tension.

## Script & pacing
- Hook (0–15 s): dramatic contradiction or event ("The biggest bank in the world… but before it was a bank, it was one man").
- Chapters with black title cards; rapid-fire facts; ironic juxtaposition humor ("mildly disappointed" → "extremely disappointed").
- Ending: ties back to present day / reflective takeaway.
- Shots average 2.5–5 s; the visual changes on each new clause of the VO.

## Official Thumbnail Blueprint (Exact Channel Standard)
- Format: 16:9, exact 1280×720 pixels (high quality JPG).
- Canvas: Flat, vibrant colored background (primary blue, royal blue, or deep red/green) with authentic colored-pencil wax crosshatch grain.
- Left Hero Character:
  - Big circular pure white head (no skin tone), black dot eyes, round glasses, and an exaggerated wide-open excited/shocked mouth.
  - Neatly parted black hair under any relevant hat (e.g. Texas cowboy hat).
  - Dark suit with white collar, red tie, and a small rectangular year/context badge on the lapel (e.g. `1938`, `1979`).
  - White hands physically acting with a primary story prop (e.g. holding chopsticks eating noodles, holding tongs pulling glowing silver bullion).
- Center-Right Typography:
  - Top Line: Giant chunky bright-yellow 3D marker text with heavy black outlines and deep black offset drop-shadow (`$1 TRILLION`, `$1 BILLION`, `1/3`).
  - Bottom Line: Bold white sans-serif handwritten text with thick black outline directly below the yellow text (`FROM NOODLES`, `IN SILVER`).
- Top-Right Props:
  - Small modern smartphone with app/chart display.
  - Minimalist glass skyscraper with bold company/location sign (`GLOBAL`, `WALL ST`) and green trees at the base.
